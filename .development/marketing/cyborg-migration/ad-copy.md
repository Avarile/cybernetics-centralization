## 1. Copy per hook

### Hook A: "The gap compounds." (`gap`, figure 3/3)

Headlines:
1. The gap compounds.
2. AI-native is the new baseline.
3. Everyone has AI. Does yours know your life?

Primary text, short:
> AI makes output cheap. What can't be copied is your own record. Start your 4-week migration.

Primary text, long:
> Everyone has AI now, so producing stuff isn't an edge anymore. What is? An AI that knows your goals, your fixes and your lessons, because you've been keeping them somewhere you own. That record compounds every week. I'm building mine in public. Start your own 4-week migration, no tech skills needed.

CTA button: **Start your migration** (platform fallback: "Learn more")

### Hook B: "Your notes aren't memory." (`notes`, figure 1/3)

Headlines:
1. Your notes aren't memory.
2. Solved it once. Forgot how.
3. One record. Not rented.

Primary text, short:
> Notes in ten apps aren't a memory. One place you own is. Week 1 of the migration starts here.

Primary text, long:
> I'd fix something, and a year later have no idea how. Notes in five apps, goals gone by March. The fix wasn't more notes. It was one organised place for my life that I own, and that any AI can read. Get the 4-week checklist and the starter template.

CTA button: **Get the checklist**

### Hook C: "Swap the mind. Keep the memory." (`swap`, figure 2/3)

Headlines:
1. Swap the mind. Keep the memory.
2. Use any AI without giving it your life.
3. AI is the processor, not the vault.

Primary text, short:
> Your memory shouldn't live inside one assistant. Keep it in a place you own and plug any AI into it.

Primary text, long:
> When you tell an AI about your life, that context usually lives in its memory, where you can't see it, organise it or take it with you. I keep mine in my own place, behind keys I can take back, and plug in whichever AI fits the job. Start your 4-week migration.

CTA button: **Start your migration**
