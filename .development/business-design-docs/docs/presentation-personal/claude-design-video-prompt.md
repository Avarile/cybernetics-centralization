Create a 150-second animated explainer video titled "Cyborg Migration".

## Purpose and audience
- Audience: a general online audience. They are curious about AI, mostly non-technical, and scrolling on their phones.
- Goal: convince viewers that becoming AI-native is now necessary to stay competitive, show them the three-part way to do it, and get them to start their own 4-week migration and follow the creator's build series.
- Core message (viewers must remember this): "My memory. Any mind. Closed loops." Supporting line: "The gap compounds."
- Tone: calm, confident, personal and urgent, but never fearful. A build log, not a hype reel. No buzzword clutter.

## Format
- Master: 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 150 seconds, 8 scenes.
- Also deliver a 9:16 (1080×1920) cut for short-form platforms with the same scenes and captions. Recompose each scene vertically: captions in the upper half, visual in the lower-middle. Do not just crop the master.
- Text only: no voiceover and no people speaking. All meaning is carried by on-screen captions and motion, so the video must work with the sound off.
- Optional: a minimal, ambient electronic music bed at low volume. No sound effects that depend on being heard.
- Captions: at most 2 lines on screen at once, each held long enough to read twice (about 3 seconds per short line). Size them for phones: at least 56px in 16:9 and 64px in 9:16.
- Keep the bottom 12% of both formats free of captions and key visuals, because platform UI and captions sit there.

## Visual style (strict, matching the companion presentation)
- Black and white only. No colour accents. Pure black background (#000000), white (#FFFFFF) foreground, and greys for hierarchy and depth (#1A1A1A, #444444, #888888, #CCCCCC).
- Emphasis comes from contrast, scale, weight and motion, never from colour.
- Visual grammar (keep it consistent through the whole video):
  - MEMORY / MY DATA = solid, filled white shapes and glowing white dots.
  - AI MODELS = outlined, hollow shapes (thin white strokes, never filled). This contrast shows that AI is the processor and my data is the asset.
  - DATA FLOW = small white points of light travelling along thin lines.
- THE CYBORG FIGURE: a single head-and-shoulders human figure, drawn as a thin grey outline with no face details. It gains three parts over the video:
  - a solid white core in the chest (MEMORY)
  - an outlined ring at the head (PROCESSING), never filled because it can be swapped
  - white circuit lines joining the head and the core in a closed loop (LEARNING)

  A small "MIGRATION" bar under the figure fills 0/3 → 3/3.
- Typography: one clean geometric sans serif (Inter, Helvetica Neue or similar). Large, generous whitespace and tight kerning on headlines. Section titles use small uppercase letter-spaced labels such as "MIGRATION 1/3".
- Motion: smooth and deliberate, with ease-in-out curves, a slight parallax depth and subtle film grain. No 3D gimmicks, no stock footage, no clip-art people, no robots, no real company or product logos. Show AI vendors as labelled outline shapes only.
- Recurring motifs:
  - THE LOOP: it appears broken in Scene 1, closes in Scene 7, and becomes the end-card mark in Scene 8.
  - THE GAP: two trajectory lines, one flat and one curving up, shown in Scene 2 and again in Scene 7. Shapes only, with no axes or numbers.

## Scene-by-scene

### Scene 1: The hidden cost (0:00–0:15)
Visual: Open on the empty figure outline, centred. Grey fragments drift away from it, disconnected: note-app cards, bookmark stars, a banking screen, calendar blocks, chat bubbles, a sticky note. A white arrow draws itself in a circle labelled PLAN → DO → … and fades out before it comes back to the start. The loop stays open.
Captions:
- "AI-native is the new baseline. Are you?"
- "Solved it once. Forgot how."
- "Goals set in January. Gone by March."
- "I can't improve what I don't capture."
(Hold the last line on screen alone, in larger type.)

### Scene 2: The stakes, and the trap (0:15–0:33)
Visual: Two figure outlines stand side by side. Above each one, a loop turns: the left loop stays open, and the right loop closes and brightens with each lap. Under them, two thin lines trace their paths. The left line is flat, and the right line curves upward. A faint grey band labelled "THE GAP" widens between them.
Then a cut to the trap. An outlined AI shape produces a glossy answer card, which cracks and crumbles into grey dust. Then white memory dots are pulled out of a figure into an outlined box labelled "ASSISTANT MEMORY", and a lock clicks shut.
Captions:
- "AI makes output cheap. Your own record isn't."
- "The gap compounds."
- "But AI without your data only looks right."
- "And memory inside one assistant is rented."

### Scene 3: Part 1, memory (0:33–0:51)
Visual: The drifting fragments from Scene 1 are pulled back and condense into one solid white core in the figure's chest. Labelled nodes connect around it with thin lines: GOALS, PROJECTS, TASKS, KNOWLEDGE, PEOPLE, MONEY, FRAMEWORKS, LOG. The core splits subtly into two layers, labelled "FACTS: what happened" and "NOW: what I'm doing". The migration bar fills to 1/3.
Captions:
- Label: "MIGRATION 1/3 — DATA FIRST"
- "One memory. Owned by me."
- "Every plan, note, decision and result is linked."

### Scene 4: Part 2, processing (0:51–1:09)
Visual: Outlined rings labelled "MODEL A", "MODEL B" and "ANY FUTURE MODEL" dock at the figure's head one at a time, connect to the core with a flowing line, then undock and are replaced. The core never changes or dims. One ring stays, and the migration bar fills to 2/3. Then one record leaves the core and fans out into five outputs: REVIEW, PLAN, CODE, IMAGE, VIDEO.
Captions:
- Label: "MIGRATION 2/3 — AI AGNOSTIC"
- "AI is the processor, not the vault."
- "Swap the mind. Keep the memory."

### Scene 5: My real setup (1:09–1:33)
Visual: A clean schema diagram draws itself. Small solid white rounded rectangles with monospace labels: `goals` → `projects` → `tasks` down the centre, `knowledges` linked to all three, `contacts` on the left, a "finance" cluster of six small boxes on the right, and `project_frameworks` and `auditlog` at the bottom. Small grey label: "data-centre".
Then a minimal chat input box appears in front of the diagram and types: "How much did I spend on food in 6 months?" A step trail ticks off below it: "Read" → "Apply framework" → "Write" → "Logged." As it runs, light-dots travel from the chat to the relevant tables and back, and the `auditlog` box gains a glowing dot.
Captions:
- "My actual base. Every table, the same shape."
- "I just ask. It reads, applies, writes and logs."
Note: show table names only. Never show record values, amounts, people's names, credentials or tokens.

### Scene 6: Clarity without effort (1:33–1:51)
Visual: A quick, rhythmic three-part montage, each part pulling light from the glowing trails in the core.
  a) A morning brief document assembles itself line by line (grey placeholder bars, no readable content).
  b) A clean monochrome chart renders: bars and a trend line, with no axis values.
  c) A knowledge tree expands branch by branch, with nodes lighting up as they're linked.
Captions:
- "A morning brief that writes itself."
- "Any question about my life, answered from facts."
- "Never solve the same problem twice."

### Scene 7: Part 3, the loop closes (1:51–2:09)
Visual: The broken arrow from Scene 1 returns and this time completes the circle: PLAN → DO → CHECK → ACT → back to PLAN. White circuit lines join the head ring and the core, completing the figure. The arrow does three laps, labelled "WEEK 1", "WEEK 2" and "WEEK 3", each faster than the last. The ring gets thicker and brighter, and the core grows. The migration bar fills to 3/3. The upward-curving line from Scene 2 reappears under the figure and extends with each lap.
Captions:
- Label: "MIGRATION 3/3 — CLOSE THE LOOP"
- "Every week feeds the next."
- "Mistakes become memory."
- "That's the compounding."

### Scene 8: Lean and safe, then the call (2:09–2:30)
Visual: Everything else fades away, leaving only the complete figure: its outlined head ring and solid core, joined by circuit lines. Small key icons appear, and each key lights only its own segment of the core, while the other segments stay dim.
Captions:
- "No app to build. No vendor holding my memory."
- "Just AI and my own database."
Then a cut to black. The closed loop draws itself as a white ring mark in the centre, with the complete figure small inside it, and the title appears below:
- Title: "Cyborg Migration"
- Subtitle: "My memory. Any mind. Closed loops."
Final card, held for 5 seconds:
- "AI-native is the new baseline. Start your 4-week migration."

## Guardrails
- Do not invent statistics, percentages or productivity numbers. The gap is shown as shape only.
- Do not show real software logos or brand names. Use generic labels. "data-centre" and the table names in Scene 5 are the creator's own schema names, and they are allowed.
- Never render personal data: no amounts, names, credentials, tokens, or realistic placeholder values.
- Urgent, not fearful: no robots, no job-loss imagery, no dystopian visuals.
- Do not use colour anywhere. Black, white and grey only.
- Keep every caption exactly as written above. Do not add extra slogans.
- Make sure each scene transition is motivated by the visuals (fragments become the core, the parts complete the figure, the broken arrow becomes the closed loop) so the video reads as one continuous story.
