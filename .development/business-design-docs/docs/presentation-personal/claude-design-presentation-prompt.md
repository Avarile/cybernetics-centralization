Create an animated slide-show video titled "Cyborg Migration: Presentation Edition".

## How this video will be used (read first)
- This is long-form online video. A creator talks over it with a voiceover and an optional face-cam bubble, and it's published for a general online audience. It is a set of animated slides, not a standalone explainer.
- That means:
  - The voiceover does the explaining. The screen carries only headlines, keywords and visuals. Never write full sentences where a keyword will do.
  - Each slide has two phases:
    1. BUILD (6–10 seconds): elements animate in, in a clear reading order.
    2. HOLD (20 seconds): the finished slide stays on screen with only subtle ambient motion (slowly drifting light-dots, a gentle pulse on the central element). Nothing new appears, so the voiceover can run long, or the editor can trim or extend the hold, without missing anything.
  - Separate slides with a short, clean transition (about 1 second). There must be no important motion during the transition.
  - Every slide shows a small slide counter in the bottom-right corner (for example "03 / 15") in grey. It doubles as the chapter number.
- No voiceover, no music and no sound effects are baked in. Audio is added in the edit.

## Format
- 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 15 slides, roughly 7–7.5 minutes in total.
- Viewers watch on phones. Headlines are at least 72px, keywords at least 40px, and no slide has more than about 18 words.
- Safe zones, on every slide:
  - Keep the bottom 12% of the frame free of text and key visuals, because platform captions sit there. The slide counter is the only exception.
  - Keep the bottom-left area (25% of the width × 30% of the height) empty, for a face-cam bubble.
- The held frame of slide 01 must work as a thumbnail: the figure, the half-formed core, and the word "CYBORG" large and legible at small sizes.

## Visual style (strict, matching the companion explainer video)
- Black and white only. Pure black background (#000000), white (#FFFFFF) foreground, and greys for hierarchy (#1A1A1A, #444444, #888888, #CCCCCC). No colour anywhere.
- Emphasis comes from contrast, scale, weight and motion.
- Visual grammar (consistent on every slide):
  - MEMORY / MY DATA = solid, filled white shapes and glowing white dots. The central "core" is a solid white circle.
  - AI MODELS = outlined, hollow shapes (thin white strokes, never filled).
  - DATA FLOW = small white points of light travelling along thin lines.
  - THE LOOP = a circular arrow, the recurring motif. It is open (broken) until slide 11 and closed from slide 11 onward.
- THE CYBORG FIGURE (the through-line of the whole video):
  - A single human figure, head and shoulders only, drawn as a thin grey outline. It's simple and geometric, and has no face details.
  - It gains parts as the video progresses:
    - MEMORY: a solid white core in the chest. It is added on slide 06.
    - PROCESSING: a thin outlined ring at the head. It is added on slide 09. It is always outlined, never filled, because the AI is a swappable part.
    - LEARNING: thin white circuit lines that join the head ring and the core in a closed loop. They are added on slide 11.
  - A small three-segment bar under the figure, labelled "MIGRATION", fills one segment per part: 0/3 → 1/3 → 2/3 → 3/3.
  - Once a part is added, it stays on every later appearance of the figure.
- Typography: one clean geometric sans serif (Inter, Helvetica Neue or similar). Small uppercase letter-spaced section labels above headlines (for example "MIGRATION 1/3").
- Layout: generous whitespace and a consistent grid. Headline top-left, visual centre or right, with the safe zones respected.
- No stock footage, no clip-art people, no real logos or product names. Show AI vendors as labelled outline shapes: "MODEL A", "MODEL B", "ANY FUTURE MODEL".
- Do not invent statistics, percentages or productivity figures. Show trajectories as shapes only, with no axes or numbers.

## Guardrails
- Use the on-screen text exactly as written. Do not add slogans, sentences or numbers.
- Every slide's final state must be fully readable on its own as a still frame, including on a phone.
- Black, white and grey only, on every slide.
- No real product names or logos on screen. "data-centre" and the table names on slides 07–08 are the creator's own schema names, and they are allowed.
- Slides 07–08 show structure only. Never render record values, amounts, people's names, credentials or tokens, not even as placeholder "realistic" data.
- Urgency without fear: no doom imagery, no robots replacing people, and no "AI will take your job" visuals.
