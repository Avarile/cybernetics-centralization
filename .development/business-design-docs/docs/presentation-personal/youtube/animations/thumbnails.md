# Thumbnail Graphic Plates

Paste `00-shared-spec.md` first (for the style only; these are stills, not animations).

Create five **still** graphic plates for video thumbnails, at 1280×720 PNG with a transparent background, plus a version on pure black. The presenter's photo is added afterwards on the **left 45%** of the frame, so keep everything within the **right 55%**, except the headline word, which may extend into the centre.

Common rules:
- Black, white and greys only, with the same cyborg figure style as the animations, but bolder: a 5 px stroke and a stronger glow on solid parts so it reads at small sizes.
- At most 3 words of text, extra-bold, at least 150 px tall, white with a subtle dark outline for legibility over the photo.
- No small details. It must read clearly at 320×180.

| Plate | Figure state | Extra element | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Outline only, with an empty chest core flickering faintly; a ring around it, almost closed | none | "CYBORG?" |
| T2 | Solid glowing core in the chest | Grey note blobs heaped at the figure's feet | "NOT MEMORY" |
| T3 | Solid core, with an outlined ring at the head | Three outlined rings queued above the head, and a key icon beside the figure | "SWAP THE AI" |
| T4 | Solid core and head ring | A large outlined chat bubble reading "How did I fix this last time?" (smaller text is allowed here), and a small clock icon | "I JUST ASK" |
| T5 | Complete: core, head ring and closed circuit loop; a glowing closed ring around it | A gently rising line beneath the figure | "IT LEARNS" |
