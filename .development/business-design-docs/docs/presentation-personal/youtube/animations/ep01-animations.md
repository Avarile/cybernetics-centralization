# Episode 1 Animations: "I'm Turning Myself Into a Cyborg. Here's the Plan."

Paste `00-shared-spec.md` first. Render each clip below as a separate file, following the shared spec (IN / BUILD / HOLD loop / OUT, the named layout, transparent or pure-black background).

Recurring clips for this episode: **G-01a** (title sting), **G-03** (text below), **G-04** (text below), **G-06a** ("#2 MEMORY").

---

### E1-01 Open loops (SIDE, BUILD 8 s)
**Script cue:** [0:40] "My life was full of open loops"
**Build:** The cyborg figure outline sits in the centre of the SIDE area. Grey everyday fragments drift slowly away from it in different directions, one after another: a sticky note, a phone screenshot, a receipt, a calendar square, a chat bubble, a bookmark star. They stay disconnected. Then a circular arrow draws itself around the figure (PLAN → DO → …), slows down and fades out before it gets back to the start. The gap stays open.
**Text (appears last):** "OPEN LOOPS"
**Hold:** The fragments drift very slowly, and the open gap in the arrow flickers faintly.

### E1-02 Everyone has AI (SIDE, BUILD 5 s)
**Script cue:** [1:40] "Everyone has AI now…"
**Build:** A row of six identical small outlined figures appears. Above each one, an identical small outlined AI ring lights up at the same moment. Every figure produces an identical grey document card. The cards stack up into one big, uniform pile.
**Text:** "EVERYONE HAS AI" → after a beat, a second line: "SO WHAT'S DIFFERENT?"
**Hold:** The pile gently settles.

### E1-03 The gap grows (SIDE, BUILD 10 s)
**Script cue:** [1:40] "…that stuff adds up… the gap… grows. Every week."
**Build:** Two cyborg figures stand side by side, the same size. Under each one, a thin horizontal line starts at the same point.
- Left figure: an arrow loop above its head stays open, the figure stays grey, and its line stays flat.
- Right figure: its loop closes, and with each lap a solid core in its chest grows brighter, while its line curves gently upward.
A faint grey band between the two lines is labelled "THE GAP", and it widens with each lap. Three laps. No axes and no numbers.
**Text:** "THE GAP GROWS" (top), "EVERY WEEK" (small, under it)
**Hold:** The right line keeps creeping up very slowly, and the band keeps widening slightly. Loop it seamlessly by gently pulsing the band rather than growing it forever.

### E1-04 Confident guesser (SIDE, BUILD 6 s)
**Script cue:** [3:00] "Ask an AI about your life without telling it anything…"
**Build:** An outlined AI ring produces a glossy-looking white answer card that slides to the centre. Hairline cracks spread across it, and it crumbles into grey dust, because there's nothing underneath it.
**Text:** "SOUNDS RIGHT." → "FITS NOBODY."
**Hold:** The dust drifts slowly downward.

### E1-05 Rented memory (SIDE, BUILD 6 s)
**Script cue:** [3:00] "…where does that go? Into that one app's memory."
**Build:** A figure with a few glowing white dots in its chest. The dots get pulled out along a line into a large outlined box labelled "AI APP". The box closes, and a padlock icon clicks shut on it. The figure is left grey and empty.
**Text:** "RENTED MEMORY"
**Hold:** The padlock glints softly every few seconds.

### E1-06 Already a little cyborg (SIDE, BUILD 6 s)
**Script cue:** [3:50] "Your phone remembers phone numbers…"
**Build:** The figure outline. Small thin lines connect outward from its head to two simple line icons that appear beside it: a phone and a calendar. A tiny light-dot travels along each line.
**Text:** "YOU'RE ALREADY OUTSOURCING"
**Hold:** The dots keep travelling slowly.

### E1-07 The three parts (SIDE, BUILD 9 s)
**Script cue:** [3:50] "…a cyborg needs three parts." / [4:40] "The 3 parts"
**Build:** The figure is centred. Three parts appear one at a time as **dashed** outlines, each with a label line out to the right:
1. A circle in the chest: "MEMORY"
2. A ring at the head: "AI"
3. A loop of lines joining head and chest: "LEARNING"
**Hold:** The three dashed parts breathe gently in sequence.

### E1-08 Part one: memory (SIDE, BUILD 6 s)
**Script cue:** [4:40] "Part one: memory."
**Build:** The figure from E1-07. The dashed chest circle fills into a solid white core. Small labelled dots appear around it and connect to it with thin lines: GOALS, PLANS, TO-DOS, HOW-TOS, PEOPLE, MONEY.
**Text:** "ONE PLACE. YOURS."
**Hold:** Light-dots flow slowly along the lines into the core.

### E1-09 Part two: AI (SIDE, BUILD 8 s)
**Script cue:** [4:40] "Part two: processing."
**Build:** The figure with a solid core. Outlined rings labelled "AI A", "AI B" and "ANY FUTURE AI" dock onto the head one after another. Each one connects to the core with a flowing line, then undocks. The core never changes. One ring stays.
**Text:** "SWAP THE BRAIN." → "KEEP THE MEMORY."
**Hold:** The rings keep slowly cycling on and off the head.

### E1-10 Part three: learning (SIDE, BUILD 9 s)
**Script cue:** [4:40] "Part three: learning."
**Build:** The figure with a core and a head ring. A circular arrow, PLAN → DO → CHECK → ACT, draws around the figure and **closes**. White circuit lines join the ring and the core. The loop goes round three times, and each lap makes the ring thicker and the core slightly bigger. The MIGRATION bar under the figure previews 3/3, then fades back to 0/3.
**Text:** "EVERY WEEK, A LITTLE BETTER"
**Hold:** The loop turns slowly.

### E1-11 I just ask (FULL, BUILD 9 s)
**Script cue:** [6:20] "What it looks like for me"
**Build:** A minimal outlined chat box in the centre. Three questions type themselves, one after another, each clearing before the next:
1. "How much did I spend eating out in 6 months?"
2. "How did I fix the Wi-Fi last time?"
3. "Plan my week around my goals."
After each one, a light-dot travels from the chat box to a small solid core on the right and back, leaving a glowing trail inside the core.
**Text (after the third question):** "I ASK. IT REMEMBERS."
**Hold:** Prompt 3 stays, and the core glows gently.

### E1-12 Simpler than it sounds (SIDE, BUILD 6 s)
**Script cue:** [7:10] "It's simpler than it sounds"
**Build:** Three outlined boxes, "CODING", "NEW APP" and "TECH SKILLS", each get a clean diagonal strike and fade away. What remains: one outlined AI ring and one solid core, joined by a single line.
**Text:** "AN AI + ONE PLACE YOU OWN"
**Hold:** A dot travels slowly back and forth along the line.

### E1-13 The 4-week challenge (SIDE, BUILD 8 s)
**Script cue:** [7:40] "The 4-week challenge"
**Build:** A vertical timeline draws from top to bottom, with four nodes appearing in turn:
- "WEEK 1 · MEMORY"
- "WEEK 2 · CONNECT AN AI"
- "WEEK 3 · LIVE ON IT"
- "WEEK 4 · LOOK BACK"
A small cyborg figure sits at the top of the timeline. It gains one part at each node, and is complete at week 4.
**Text (top):** "DO IT WITH ME"
**Hold:** The week 1 node pulses gently.
