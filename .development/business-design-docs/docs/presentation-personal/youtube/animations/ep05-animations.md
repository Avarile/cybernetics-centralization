# Episode 5 Animations: "The Loop: How My Life Gets a Little Better Every Week"

Paste `00-shared-spec.md` first. Render each clip below as a separate file, following the shared spec.

Recurring clips for this episode: **G-01e** (title sting), **G-02d** (2/3 → 3/3), **G-03**, **G-04**, **G-05** (screen frame for the demos), **G-06e** ("WATCH THE WHOLE MIGRATION").

---

### E5-01 Review from memory vs. from facts (SIDE, BUILD 8 s)
**Script cue:** [0:00] cold open, "the first weekly review… that didn't lie to me"
**Build:** Two review sheets side by side.
- Left, "FROM MEMORY": a sheet whose lines are wavy and blurry, drawn out of a soft grey thought cloud.
- Right, "FROM FACTS": a sheet whose lines are crisp and straight, each connected by a thin line down to a small solid dot in a row labelled "WHAT HAPPENED".
The left sheet fades to grey, and the right sheet brightens.
**Text:** "A STORY" (left) / "THE TRUTH" (right)
**Hold:** Dots pulse along the right sheet's connections.

### E5-02 The two steps everyone skips (SIDE, BUILD 9 s)
**Script cue:** [0:40] "Why most plans never get better"
**Build:** A circle of four labels: "PLAN", "DO", "CHECK", "ACT". An arrow travels PLAN → DO brightly, then turns faint and dashed through CHECK and ACT, which stay grey (#444444), and it never reconnects to PLAN. A beat. Then CHECK and ACT light up white, the arrow becomes solid all the way round, and the circle closes.
**Text:** "MOST OF US SKIP CHECK + ACT" → (once it closes) "NOW IT'S EASY"
**Hold:** The closed loop turns slowly.

### E5-03 Three loops, three speeds (SIDE, BUILD 6 s)
**Script cue:** [0:40] "I run three loops"
**Build:** Three concentric rings draw in from the inside out. Each one turns at a different speed and has a label:
- Inner, fastest: "EVERY WEEK"
- Middle: "EVERY PROJECT"
- Outer, slowest: "THE SYSTEM ITSELF"
**Hold:** All three rings keep turning at their own speeds.

### E5-04 The honest weekly review (SIDE, BUILD 10 s)
**Script cue:** [1:50] "Loop 1: the honest weekly review"
**Build:** An outlined review sheet. Four sections fill in one at a time, each with grey placeholder lines and a small icon:
1. "WHAT MOVED" (an upward arrow)
2. "WHAT GOT STUCK" (a pause icon). Make this section slightly heavier, as the uncomfortable part.
3. "WHAT I DIDN'T PLAN" (a diary icon)
4. "3 FOR NEXT WEEK" (three small dots and a "?" bubble)

Then a thin arrow loops from the bottom of the sheet to a small "TOMORROW'S BRIEF" card.
**Text:** "5 MINUTES. HONEST."
**Hold:** The loop arrow pulses.

### E5-05 The "check it" step (SIDE, BUILD 9 s)
**Script cue:** [3:40] "Loop 2: finishing things properly"
**Build:** A horizontal path of four nodes: "TO DO" → "DOING" → "CHECK IT" → "DONE".
1. A dot travels along the path and tries to jump from DOING straight to DONE. A gate at "CHECK IT" stops it.
2. The dot passes through CHECK IT, which glows white and opens a small card with three lines: "DID IT WORK?", "WHAT DID I LEARN?", "WHAT CHANGES?".
3. The dot continues to DONE. The path then bends up and back to a new node, "NEXT GOAL".
**Text:** "NOTHING SKIPS THE CHECK"
**Hold:** The CHECK IT node glows gently.

### E5-06 Lessons that stick (SIDE, BUILD 10 s)
**Script cue:** [5:30] "Loop 3: lessons that stick"
**Build:** A small solid "LESSON" dot appears at the top. Three paths branch out from it to three destination cards, and the dot sends a copy along each path in turn:
1. "HOW-TO NOTE": "how to do something"
2. "MY TEMPLATES": "how I work"
3. "RECIPES": "how the AI works"
Each card gains a new white line when its copy arrives. A faint grey "THOUGHT" bubble on the side that tries to hold the lesson simply fades away, unused.
**Text:** "EVERY LESSON LANDS SOMEWHERE"
**Hold:** The three cards pulse in sequence.

### E5-07 The whole loop (FULL, BUILD 12 s)
**Script cue:** [6:50] "Why this adds up"
**Build:** A large circular flow with five stations, drawn clockwise:
1. "MEMORY" (a solid core)
2. "AI HELPS" (an outlined ring)
3. "DIARY" (a small book)
4. "WEEKLY REVIEW" (a sheet)
5. "BETTER NOTES, TEMPLATES, RECIPES" (three small stacked cards)
Then the arrow goes back to "MEMORY". Light-dots travel round the circle. The cyborg figure sits in the centre. With each lap, its core grows and the loop line thickens. Three laps.
**Text:** "AND ROUND AGAIN"
**Hold:** A slow, continuous circulation.

### E5-08 The gap, revisited (SIDE, BUILD 8 s)
**Script cue:** [6:50] "This is why the gap grows."
**Build:** The same scene as `E1-03` from episode 1: two figures, one flat line and one upward-curving line, with "THE GAP" widening between them. This time the right-hand figure is the complete cyborg: core, head ring and closed loop.
**Text:** "BETTER EVERY WEEK. AND IT STAYS."
**Hold:** Same as E1-03.

### E5-09 Before vs. after (SIDE, BUILD 10 s)
**Script cue:** [7:50] "4 weeks: honest results"
**Build:** A two-column table. The header row reads "BEFORE" (grey) and "NOW" (white). Rows slide in one at a time, with the left cell in grey and the right cell in white:
| BEFORE | NOW |
|---|---|
| Notes everywhere | One place |
| Fixes forgotten | Fixes kept |
| Guessing about money | Answers in seconds |
| Reviews skipped | Honest reviews |
| Same mistakes | Lessons that stick |
**Hold:** Static, with a faint pulse on the right column.

### E5-10 The cyborg is complete (SIDE, BUILD 8 s)
**Script cue:** [8:50] "The cyborg is complete. What's next."
**Build:** The figure assembles all three parts in sequence: the core fills, the ring docks, and the circuit loop closes. The MIGRATION bar fills to 3/3, and the outer ring around the figure closes and glows. A beat, then small grey text appears beneath: "SEASON 2".
**Text:** "MEMORY · AI · LEARNING"
**Hold:** The ring pulses gently.
