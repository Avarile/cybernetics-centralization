# Episode 3 Animations: "Use Any AI Without Giving It Your Life"

Paste `00-shared-spec.md` first. Render each clip below as a separate file, following the shared spec.

Recurring clips for this episode: **G-01c** (title sting), **G-02b** (1/3 → 2/3), **G-03**, **G-04**, **G-05** (screen frame for the demos), **G-06c** ("#4 A DAY").

---

### E3-01 Same question, same answer (FULL, BUILD 7 s)
**Script cue:** [0:00] cold open (use alongside or instead of the split-screen demo)
**Build:** Two outlined AI rings, "AI A" on the left and "AI B" on the right, with one solid white core between them at the bottom. A line connects each ring to the core. The same question bubble appears under both rings at once: "WHAT'S LEFT FOR MUM'S BIRTHDAY?" Light-dots travel from both rings down to the core and back. Identical answer cards appear under both, each with three short grey lines.
**Text:** "SAME MEMORY. ANY AI."
**Hold:** Dots keep flowing both ways.

### E3-02 Three reasons (SIDE, BUILD 9 s)
**Script cue:** [0:35] "Why the AI shouldn't own your memory"
**Build:** Three cards appear in a vertical stack, one at a time, each with a simple line icon:
1. A fast-forward icon: "AI CHANGES FAST"
2. Three different outlined shapes: "DIFFERENT AIs, DIFFERENT JOBS"
3. A small house: "IT'S YOUR LIFE"
**Hold:** Each card pulses gently in turn. **Also render** single-highlight versions (3 s each, with the other cards dimmed).

### E3-03 Swap the brain, keep the memory (SIDE, BUILD 8 s)
**Script cue:** [0:35] "the AI is the brain you rent. The memory is yours."
**Build:** The cyborg figure with a solid core. Outlined rings dock at the head one after another and undock: "AI A", "AI B", "ANY FUTURE AI". The core stays perfectly steady and bright the whole time. Each time a ring undocks, a small label "RENTED" flickers beside it. A steady small label beside the core reads "YOURS".
**Text:** "SWAP THE BRAIN. KEEP THE MEMORY."
**Hold:** A calm, continuous cycle of rings docking and undocking.

### E3-04 The USB idea (SIDE, BUILD 10 s)
**Script cue:** [2:00] "How it plugs in (the USB idea)"
**Build:**
1. A simple line drawing of a USB plug and socket appears, and the plug clicks into the socket. Label: "ONE PLUG FITS ALL".
2. It morphs into the system: an outlined AI ring on the left, a solid memory core on the right, and between them a small outlined plug-and-socket shape labelled "PLUG".
3. The AI plugs in. A question dot ("WHAT ARE MY GOALS?") travels to the core, and a small answer dot comes back. The AI does **not** get a copy: show a ghost copy of the core trying to form next to the AI, then dissolving.
4. The AI unplugs. A different outlined AI shape slides in and plugs into the same plug.
**Text (end):** "IT ASKS. IT NEVER KEEPS A COPY."
**Hold:** The second AI's questions and answers flow gently.

### E3-05 Keys you can take back (SIDE, BUILD 10 s)
**Script cue:** [3:00] "Keys you can take back"
**Build:** A solid memory core divided into four labelled segments: "GOALS", "NOTES", "PEOPLE" and "MONEY". Three outlined AI rings sit around it, each holding a small key icon.
1. Each key slides into the core and lights **only** its own segment. The other segments stay dim. "MONEY" has its own separate key, and only one AI holds it.
2. One AI's key turns grey, crumbles away, and that AI's line disconnects. Everything else stays lit.

Three short rules appear one by one beside the core:
- "ONE KEY PER APP"
- "ONLY OPEN WHAT'S NEEDED"
- "TAKE IT BACK ANYTIME"
**Hold:** The remaining keys glint softly.

### E3-06 Connecting is copy and paste (SIDE, BUILD 6 s)
**Script cue:** [3:50] "Connecting it"
**Build:** Three simple steps in a row, each with a line icon, appearing in order and joined by small arrows:
1. A key icon: "MAKE A KEY"
2. A clipboard icon: "PASTE IT IN"
3. A chat bubble: "ASK"
**Text:** "A FEW MINUTES"
**Hold:** The chat bubble pulses.

### E3-07 What a recipe is (SIDE, BUILD 8 s)
**Script cue:** [4:40] "Recipes: teach it once"
**Build:** An outlined recipe card titled "RECIPE". Four lines write themselves onto it, each with a small icon:
- "WHEN" (a clock)
- "WHERE" (a pin)
- "HOW" (a list)
- "WRITE IT IN THE DIARY" (a small book)
Then an outlined AI ring reads the card: a scan line passes over it, and the ring glows.
**Text:** "TEACH IT ONCE"
**Hold:** The scan line repeats slowly.

### E3-08 One message, filed properly (FULL, BUILD 12 s)
**Script cue:** [5:30] "Recipe 1: 'remember this'" (between or over the demo)
**Build:**
1. A long grey chat bubble at the top holds a rambly message, shown as grey text lines with two phrases highlighted in white: "fixed the Wi-Fi: router auto-update at 3am" and "remind me to call the internet company".
2. The message splits. The first phrase flies down-left and becomes a solid white HOW-TO card, which connects with a line to an existing card, "HOW I RESET THE ROUTER". The second phrase flies down-right and becomes a TO-DO card with a small calendar icon and the label "THIS WEEKEND", attached under a PLAN card, "FIX HOME WI-FI".
3. A small DIARY strip at the bottom gains a new line.
**Text (end):** "ONE MESSAGE. FILED. CONNECTED."
**Hold:** The links glow gently.

### E3-09 The weekly review recipe (SIDE, BUILD 9 s)
**Script cue:** [6:40] "Recipe 2: the weekly review"
**Build:** A light-dot sweeps across a row of seven small day squares (the past week) and collects small dots from each one. The dots gather into an outlined review sheet with four sections that fill in one by one:
- "WHAT MOVED"
- "WHAT GOT STUCK"
- "WHAT I DIDN'T PLAN"
- "3 FOR NEXT WEEK"
A small "?" bubble on the last section shows that it asks first.
**Text:** "FROM WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED"
**Hold:** The sheet glows gently.
