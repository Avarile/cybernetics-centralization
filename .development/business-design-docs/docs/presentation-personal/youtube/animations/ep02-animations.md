# Episode 2 Animations: "Your Notes Aren't Memory. Here's What Is."

Paste `00-shared-spec.md` first. Render each clip below as a separate file, following the shared spec.

Recurring clips for this episode: **G-01b** (title sting), **G-02a** (0/3 → 1/3), **G-03**, **G-04**, **G-05** (screen frame for the demo), **G-06b** ("#3 PROCESSING").

---

### E2-01 Thousands of notes (SIDE, BUILD 5 s)
**Script cue:** [0:00] cold open, "I had [N] notes on my phone…"
**Build:** A simple outlined phone. Grey note cards spill out of it and pile up into a messy heap beside it. An outlined AI ring hovers over the heap and pulses, searching. A small "?" appears.
**Text:** "A PILE, NOT A MEMORY"
**Hold:** The AI ring keeps slowly searching over the heap.

### E2-02 Pile vs. memory (SIDE, BUILD 10 s)
**Script cue:** [0:35] "A pile vs. a memory"
**Build:** The area splits into two halves with a thin vertical rule.
- Left, labelled "PILE": grey note blobs in a heap, all the same shape, with text-like lines running everywhere.
- Right, labelled "MEMORY": the same blobs lift off the left side, travel across, and settle as neat white cards with small type labels: GOAL, PLAN, TO-DO, HOW-TO. Thin lines then connect them into a chain: TO-DO → PLAN → GOAL, plus a side line TO-DO → HOW-TO.
**Hold:** A light-dot travels along the chain on the right. The left side stays still.

### E2-03 The 4 rules (SIDE, BUILD 10 s)
**Script cue:** [2:00] "The 4 rules of a good memory"
**Build:** Four rule cards appear one at a time in a 2×2 grid. Each has a large number, a simple line icon and a short label:
1. "ONE PLACE" (icon: a single box)
2. "EVERYTHING HAS A TYPE" (icon: a tag)
3. "CONNECT THINGS" (icon: two dots joined by a line)
4. "KEEP WHAT HAPPENED" (icon: a clock with a check)
**Hold:** Each card pulses gently in turn. **Also render** four extra 3 s versions where only card 1, 2, 3 or 4 is fully white and the rest are dimmed to #444444, so the editor can highlight each rule as it's spoken.

### E2-04 Connections answer "why?" (SIDE, BUILD 7 s)
**Script cue:** [2:00] rule three, "'why did I do it that way?'"
**Build:** The chain GOAL → PLAN → TO-DO, with a HOW-TO card attached to the TO-DO. A small outlined question bubble reads "WHY?". A light-dot travels from the bubble backwards through the chain, lighting each card as it passes, and ends on the HOW-TO card, which glows.
**Text:** "ASK 'WHY?' MONTHS LATER"
**Hold:** The HOW-TO card glows softly.

### E2-05 Map of my memory (FULL, BUILD 12 s)
**Script cue:** [3:40] "A map of my memory"
**Build:** A clean map draws itself, using rounded solid white boxes with plain-English labels and thin connecting lines:
- **Centre column, top to bottom:** "GOALS" → "PLANS" → "TO-DOS"
- **Right:** "HOW-TOS & NOTES", linked to all three centre boxes
- **Left:** "PEOPLE"
- **Bottom right:** "MONEY", a small cluster of three stacked boxes
- **Bottom row:** "MY TEMPLATES" and "DIARY"

The boxes appear in the order the presenter mentions them: centre column, right, left, bottom right, bottom row. Light-dots travel along the links.
**Hold:** A slow ambient flow of dots along all the links.
**Also render** a 4 s version where "GOALS", "PLANS", "TO-DOS" and "HOW-TOS & NOTES" stay bright and everything else dims to #444444, for "Start with the middle and the right."

### E2-06 Pages that know each other (SIDE, BUILD 6 s)
**Script cue:** [5:00] "a very organised notebook, where the pages know about each other"
**Build:** A simple outlined open notebook. Its pages lift out one by one and turn into small solid white cards (GOAL, PLAN, TO-DO, HOW-TO), which link up with thin lines as they land.
**Text:** "A NOTEBOOK WHERE PAGES KNOW EACH OTHER"
**Hold:** A light-dot loops through the links.

### E2-07 Your 3 things go in (SIDE, BUILD 9 s)
**Script cue:** [6:00] "Putting your 3 things in"
**Build:** On the left of the SIDE area, three grey sticky notes are stacked: "RESET ROUTER", "HIKING FITNESS" and "TAX CLAIMS". One by one, each flies right and transforms:
- "RESET ROUTER" becomes a HOW-TO card.
- "HIKING FITNESS" becomes a GOAL card, with a PLAN card and three small TO-DO cards growing beneath it.
- "TAX CLAIMS" becomes a HOW-TO card, which then links to a TO-DO card, "SORT OUT TAX RETURN".
**Text:** "ORGANISED. CONNECTED."
**Hold:** The links glow softly.
