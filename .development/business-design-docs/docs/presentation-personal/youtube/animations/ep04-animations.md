# Episode 4 Animations: "I Ask, It Remembers: A Real Day"

Paste `00-shared-spec.md` first. Render each clip below as a separate file, following the shared spec.

Recurring clips for this episode: **G-01d** (title sting), **G-02c** (2/3, practice week), **G-03**, **G-04**, **G-05** (screen frame for the demos), **G-06d** ("#5 THE LOOP").

This episode is shot vlog-style at different times of day, so the clips are light and quick and mostly SIDE or CHIP.

---

### E4-01 Time stamp chips (CHIP, BUILD 1 s)
**Script cue:** at each time of day
**Build:** A small outlined clock icon draws itself, then the time types itself beside it, then a thin rule underlines it. The hold is static.
Render five versions: "07:30", "10:15", "12:40", "15:00", "21:00".
**Also render** a matching second line in small grey caps under each one: "MORNING", "MID-MORNING", "LUNCH", "AFTERNOON", "EVENING".

### E4-02 One rule this week (SIDE, BUILD 4 s)
**Script cue:** [0:35] "This week's one rule"
**Build:** Small everyday icons (a plan, a question mark, a wrench, a speech bubble) drift in from the edges and all flow into one solid white core in the centre.
**Text:** "EVERYTHING GOES THROUGH IT" / "7 DAYS"
**Hold:** A slow inflow of small dots into the core.

### E4-03 Anatomy of the morning brief (SIDE, BUILD 9 s)
**Script cue:** [1:00] "Morning: the brief"
**Build:** An outlined page titled "TODAY". Three sections fill in, one at a time, with grey placeholder lines (no readable content) and a bold label on each:
1. "PRIORITIES, BY GOAL": three lines, each with a tiny goal tag
2. "BEHIND ON": two lines, each with a small warning dot
3. "YOU SAID YOU'D…": one line, with a curved arrow looping back from the page edge to show a promise coming back
**Hold:** The section 3 arrow pulses softly. **Also render** three 3 s highlight versions, each with only one section bright.

### E4-04 The fix comes back (SIDE, BUILD 7 s)
**Script cue:** [2:20] "How did I fix this?"
**Build:** A question bubble, "HOW DID I FIX THIS?", appears. A light-dot travels back along a horizontal timeline marked "2 WEEKS AGO" and finds a solid HOW-TO card, which glows and slides forward to the present. Then a second, smaller to-do card next to it blinks: "STILL NOT DONE".
**Text:** "FOUND IN SECONDS"
**Hold:** The HOW-TO card glows, and the to-do card blinks softly.

### E4-05 Money joins memory (SIDE, BUILD 6 s)
**Script cue:** [3:30] "This week I added a money section…"
**Build:** The solid memory core with its existing small labelled nodes (GOALS, PLANS, TO-DOS, HOW-TOS, PEOPLE). A new node, "MONEY", slides in with a small coin icon, and three tiny sub-dots appear under it: "SPENDING", "CATEGORIES", "BUDGETS". It connects to the core with a line, and dots flow in.
**Text:** "SAME PLACE. SAME RULES."
**Hold:** Dots flow along the new line.

### E4-06 Where did my money go? (SIDE, BUILD 8 s)
**Script cue:** [3:30] "How much did I spend eating out…"
**Build:** A question bubble: "EATING OUT, LAST 6 MONTHS?". Below it, six vertical bars grow one by one, unlabelled except for the month initials. A horizontal dashed line marks "BUDGET". One bar crosses it, and that bar gets a small outlined marker. **No numbers anywhere.**
**Text:** "ONE QUESTION, NOT AN AFTERNOON"
**Hold:** The marker on the over-budget bar pulses gently.

### E4-07 Before I meet someone (SIDE, BUILD 9 s)
**Script cue:** [5:00] "Before I meet someone"
**Build:** A person card in the centre labelled "ALEX". Four threads extend from four directions and pull in small cards:
- "WHAT'S DECIDED"
- "WHAT'S OPEN"
- "GIFT IDEAS"
- "LAST TIME"
The cards gather into an outlined "MEETING TEMPLATE" sheet with three lines: "WHAT I WANT", "WHAT TO ASK", "WHAT TO DECIDE".
**Text:** "NEVER 'WAIT, WHAT DID WE SAY?'"
**Hold:** The sheet glows softly.

### E4-08 One message, all sorted (FULL, BUILD 10 s)
**Script cue:** [6:10] "Evening: one message, all sorted"
**Build:** A long grey chat bubble at the top. Four highlighted phrases lift out of it one by one and land as cards in four places:
- A note on "ALEX"
- A to-do "CHECK RESTAURANT BOOKED", with the calendar tag "FRI"
- An existing to-do, "CALL INTERNET COMPANY", gets a tick and fades to "DONE", while a new to-do "SET UP NEW ROUTER" appears under the "FIX HOME WI-FI" plan
- An "IDEA" card goes into a box labelled "LATER"
Finally, a "DIARY" strip at the bottom fills with today's lines.
**Text (end):** "SAID ONCE. SORTED."
**Hold:** The diary strip glows.

### E4-09 Nothing falls through (SIDE, BUILD 7 s)
**Script cue:** [7:10] "What actually changed"
**Build:**
1. Before: grey dots (small tasks, notes, promises) fall through a loose, wide-gapped outlined net and disappear.
2. After: the net tightens into a fine white mesh, and the falling dots are caught and roll into a solid core below.
Then five short labels appear around the core, one by one:
- "QUESTIONS → FACTS"
- "FIXES → KEPT"
- "PROMISES → COME BACK"
- "MONEY → CLEAR"
- "HEAD → LIGHTER"
**Text (top):** "NOTHING FALLS THROUGH"
**Hold:** An occasional dot is caught gently.
