**[SIDE]** [DECK 02]: the arrow that never closes. Then a circle, PLAN → DO → CHECK → ACT, with CHECK and ACT greyed out. → **Clip: E5-02, then E5-03 at "I run three loops"**

"Think about how most of us run our lives. We're pretty good at planning and doing. To-do lists, calendars, New Year's resolutions.

And then we skip the next two steps: **check**, meaning what actually happened, and **act**, meaning what we change because of it. Because looking back properly is hard. You'd have to piece the week together from memory and scattered apps.

But for three weeks we've been making sure nothing is scattered. Every to-do, every fix, every note is in one place, with a diary of what got done. So for the first time, looking back is *easy*. And that means the loop can finally close.

I run three loops: weekly, per project, and for the system itself."

## [5:30] LOOP 3: LESSONS THAT STICK

**[DEMO]** Lessons land in two places: a how-to note, and an updated template or recipe. → **Clip: E5-06, then the demo inside G-05**

"**Loop three: the system itself.** This is the one that really matters.

A lesson you just *think about* is forgotten by Thursday. So every lesson has to land somewhere real:

If it's about **how to do something**, it goes into a **how-to note**. [DEMO: 'Turn off router auto-updates after any reset', added to the router note.]

If it's about **how I work**, it changes one of **my templates**. [DEMO: the project template gains a line: 'Add a check-in reminder one week after finishing.']

And if it's about **the AI getting something wrong**, I fix the **recipe**. [DEMO: the 'remember this' recipe gets a line fixing the thing it kept misfiling in week three.]

So next time, the system doesn't just remember what happened. It actually *does things differently*. Mistakes turn into memory."

"Now look at what we've built in four weeks.

My memory feeds the AI. The AI helps me, and writes down what happened. The diary feeds my weekly review. The review improves my notes, my templates and my recipes. And round again.
