Create an animated slide-show video titled "The AI-Native Company: Presentation Edition".

## How this video will be used (read first)
- A presenter plays this video on a screen in front of company management and talks over it live. It is a set of animated slides, not a standalone explainer.
- That means:
  - The presenter does the explaining. The screen carries only headlines, keywords and visuals. Never write full sentences where a keyword will do.
  - Each slide has two phases:
    1. BUILD (6–10 seconds): elements animate in, in a clear reading order.
    2. HOLD (20 seconds): the finished slide stays on screen with only subtle ambient motion (slowly drifting light-dots, a gentle pulse on the central element). Nothing new appears, so the presenter can talk, or pause the video, without missing anything.
  - Separate slides with a short, clean transition (about 1 second). There must be no important motion during the transition.
  - Every slide shows a small slide counter in the bottom-right corner (for example "03 / 12") in grey, so the presenter can navigate and pause on the right slide.
- No voiceover. No music, because the presenter is speaking.

## Format
- 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 12 slides, roughly 5–6 minutes in total.
- Text sizes must be readable from the back of a meeting room: headlines at least 64px, keywords at least 36px, and never more than about 20 words on a slide.

## Visual style (strict, matching the companion explainer video)
- Black and white only. Pure black background (#000000), white (#FFFFFF) foreground, and greys for hierarchy (#1A1A1A, #444444, #888888, #CCCCCC). No colour anywhere.
- Emphasis comes from contrast, scale, weight and motion.
- Visual grammar (consistent on every slide):
  - OUR DATA = solid, filled white shapes and glowing white dots. The central "core" is a solid white circle.
  - AI MODELS = outlined, hollow shapes (thin white strokes, never filled).
  - DATA FLOW = small white points of light travelling along thin lines.
  - THE LOOP = a circular arrow, the recurring motif. It is open (broken) until slide 8 and closed from slide 8 onward.
- Typography: one clean geometric sans serif (Inter, Helvetica Neue or similar). Small uppercase letter-spaced section labels above headlines (for example "PRINCIPLE 1").
- Layout: generous whitespace and a consistent grid. Headline top-left, visual centre or right.
- No stock footage, no clip-art people, no real logos or product names (no Wrike, Claude, ChatGPT and so on). Show AI vendors as labelled outline shapes: "MODEL A", "MODEL B", "ANY FUTURE MODEL".
- Do not invent statistics, percentages, ROI figures or customer names.
