Create a 150-second animated explainer video titled "The AI-Native Company".

## Purpose and audience
- Audience: company management and decision makers. They are smart and busy, and most are not technical.
- Goal: convince leadership to approve a 4-week pilot of a new operating model with one department.
- Core message (viewers must remember this): "Our data belongs to us. AI is the worker that reads and writes it. Every process closes its loop."
- Tone: calm, confident and precise. A business case, not a hype reel. No buzzword clutter.

## Format
- 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 150 seconds, 8 scenes.
- Text only: no voiceover and no people speaking. All meaning is carried by on-screen captions and motion, so the video must work with the sound off.
- Optional: a minimal, ambient electronic music bed at low volume. No sound effects that depend on being heard.
- Captions: at most 2 lines on screen at once, each held long enough to read twice (about 3 seconds per short line).

## Visual style (strict)
- Black and white only. No colour accents. Pure black background (#000000), white (#FFFFFF) foreground, and greys for hierarchy and depth (#1A1A1A, #444444, #888888, #CCCCCC).
- Emphasis comes from contrast, scale, weight and motion, never from colour.
- Visual grammar (keep it consistent through the whole video):
  - OUR DATA = solid, filled white shapes and glowing white dots.
  - AI MODELS = outlined, hollow shapes (thin white strokes, never filled). This contrast shows that AI is the worker and our data is the asset.
  - DATA FLOW = small white points of light travelling along thin lines.
- Typography: one clean geometric sans serif (Inter, Helvetica Neue or similar). Large, generous whitespace and tight kerning on headlines. Principle titles use small uppercase letter-spaced labels such as "PRINCIPLE 1".
- Motion: smooth and deliberate, with ease-in-out curves, a slight parallax depth and subtle film grain. No 3D gimmicks, no stock footage, no clip-art people, no real company or product logos. Show AI vendors as labelled outline shapes only.
- Recurring motif: A LOOP. It appears broken in Scene 1, closes in Scene 7, and becomes the end-card mark in Scene 8.

## Scene-by-scene

### Scene 1: The hidden cost (0:00–0:15)
Visual: Fragments of work (task cards, email envelopes, chat bubbles, spreadsheet grids, a few faint person silhouettes) float apart in grey on black, disconnected. A white arrow draws itself in a circle labelled PLAN → DO → … and fades out before it comes back to the start. The loop stays open.
Captions:
- "Every week, we plan. We execute. We move on."
- "But how much do repeated mistakes cost us?"
- "How much knowledge leaves when people do?"
- "We can't answer. That is the problem."
(Hold the last line on screen alone, in larger type.)

### Scene 2: The AI trap (0:15–0:33)
Visual: An outlined AI shape generates a glossy-looking report card that slides into the centre. Hairline cracks spread across it and it shatters into grey dust, because there is nothing beneath it. Then a separate cluster of white data dots is pulled inside a large outlined box carrying an anonymous "AI PLATFORM" label, and a lock clicks shut.
Captions:
- "Everyone is buying AI."
- "Without solid data, AI produces answers that only look right."
- "Computing power without data is a dispenser of hallucinations."
- "And the data we do give it lives on someone else's platform."

### Scene 3: Principle 1, data first (0:33–0:51)
Visual: The fragments from Scene 1 are pulled to the centre and condense into one solid white core (a clean circle or cylinder). Labelled nodes orbit it and connect with thin lines into a graph: GOALS, PROJECTS, TASKS, KNOWLEDGE, CLIENTS, CAMPAIGNS, COSTS, PEOPLE. The core splits subtly into two layers, labelled "FACTS: what happened" and "OPERATIONS: what's happening now".
Captions:
- Label: "PRINCIPLE 1 — DATA FIRST"
- "One source of truth. Owned by us."
- "Every plan, input, decision and result is linked."

### Scene 4: Principle 2, AI agnostic (0:51–1:09)
Visual: The solid core sits in the centre. Outlined AI shapes labelled "MODEL A", "MODEL B" and "ANY FUTURE MODEL" dock against it one at a time, connect with a flowing line, then undock and are replaced. The core never changes or dims. Then one data record leaves the core and fans out into four outputs: REPORT, CODE, IMAGE, CAMPAIGN.
Captions:
- Label: "PRINCIPLE 2 — AI AGNOSTIC"
- "AI is the worker, not the vault."
- "Swap any model. Keep every record."
- "One dataset. Many uses."

### Scene 5: How work flows (1:09–1:33)
Visual: A minimal chat input box appears. Text types itself: "Plan follow-ups for next month's client event." Below it, a vertical sequence of steps reveals itself, each with a small white tick:
  1. "Reading: related knowledge, clients and past tasks"
  2. "Applying: the company follow-up standard"
  3. "Creating: tasks, owners and deadlines"
  4. "Recorded."
As each step runs, light-dots travel from the core to the chat and back, leaving a persistent glowing trail inside the core.
Captions:
- "Work stays a simple conversation."
- "Behind it, every step follows our standard and is recorded."

### Scene 6: Visibility without chasing (1:33–1:51)
Visual: A quick, rhythmic three-part montage, each part pulling from the glowing trails in the core.
  a) A weekly report document assembles itself line by line.
  b) A clean monochrome dashboard of bar charts and a trend line renders.
  c) A horizontal timeline replays nodes labelled WHO, WHEN, WHAT, BASED ON.
Captions:
- "Weekly reports, written from what actually happened."
- "Any dashboard, one question away."
- "Every decision, traceable."

### Scene 7: Principle 3, the loop closes (1:51–2:09)
Visual: The broken arrow from Scene 1 returns, and this time it completes the circle: PLAN → DO → CHECK → ACT → back to PLAN. Each time it goes around, the ring gets thicker and brighter and the central core grows slightly larger, showing accumulated experience. Three laps, each faster than the last.
Captions:
- Label: "PRINCIPLE 3 — CLOSE THE LOOP"
- "Every project feeds the next."
- "Every mistake becomes a lesson the system remembers."
- "The company gets smarter with every lap."

### Scene 8: Lean and safe, then the ask (2:09–2:30)
Visual: Everything else fades away, leaving only two elements: one outlined AI shape and the solid white core, joined by a single line. Small key icons appear for individual people, and each key unlocks only its own segment of the core, while the other segments stay dim.
Captions:
- "No new platform. No codebase. No vendor holding our data."
- "Just AI and our own database."
- "Personal access. Least exposure."
Then a cut to black. The closed loop draws itself as a white ring mark in the centre, and the title appears below it:
- Title: "The AI-Native Company"
- Subtitle: "Our data. Any AI. Closed loops."
Final card, held for 5 seconds:
- "Next step: approve a 4-week pilot with one department."

## Guardrails
- Do not invent statistics, percentages, ROI numbers or customer names. The message is precisely that we cannot measure these yet.
- Do not show real software logos or brand names (no Wrike, Claude, ChatGPT and so on). Use generic labels.
- Do not use colour anywhere. Black, white and grey only.
- Keep every caption exactly as written above. Do not add extra slogans.
- Make sure each scene transition is motivated by the visuals (fragments become the core, the broken arrow becomes the closed loop) so the video reads as one continuous story.
