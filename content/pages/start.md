Thanks for signing up. This page is yours: bookmark it, or keep the PDF version.

The whole idea fits in one line: *My memory. Any mind. Closed loops.* One place for your life that **you** own, any AI plugged into it, and a weekly look back that makes next week a little better.

You don't need to finish everything. Ticking one box a day is enough.

## Week 0: Notice (5 minutes)

- [ ] Write down **three things you keep forgetting, redoing or losing track of.** The fix you can never remember. The goal that keeps slipping. The thing you always have to look up again.
- [ ] Keep the list somewhere you'll see it. It's the first thing that goes into your memory.

## Week 1: Memory (migration 1/3)

Get your life into **one** place. A notebook app, a spreadsheet, or the starter template below all work. What matters is that it's one place and that it's yours.

- [ ] Set up the eight sections from the starter template (it takes about ten minutes).
- [ ] Add your **three things** from week 0.
- [ ] Add **one to three goals** for the next few months, each with a sentence on *why* it matters.
- [ ] Add the projects or plans that serve those goals.
- [ ] Write **two "how I fixed it" notes** for things you've solved before.
- [ ] Add the people you're working with or owe something to.
- [ ] Optional: add your money categories and one budget.

## Week 2: Processing (migration 2/3)

Connect an AI to that one place, so it can read and write there instead of keeping its own memory.

- [ ] Pick your AI. Any assistant that can connect to your notes or database works. You can swap it later and your memory stays put.
- [ ] Give it access to your place, using a key you can take back at any time.
- [ ] Save the **capture recipe** (below) and use it once.
- [ ] Save the **weekly review recipe** (below). You'll use it in week 4.
- [ ] Ask it one real question about your life, and check that the answer comes from your notes, not a guess.

## Week 3: Live (practice week)

Run every day through it for seven days. Don't aim for perfect; aim for every day.

- [ ] Day 1: capture something you'd normally forget.
- [ ] Day 2: ask "what should I focus on today, given my goals?"
- [ ] Day 3: when you fix something, save a "how I fixed it" note.
- [ ] Day 4: before talking to someone, ask what you last discussed or promised.
- [ ] Day 5: ask a money question you'd usually avoid.
- [ ] Day 6: update one project: what's done, what's next.
- [ ] Day 7: rest. Just glance at what got captured this week.

## Week 4: Loop (migration 3/3)

Your first honest weekly review, built from the record, not from your memory of the week.

- [ ] Run the **weekly review recipe**.
- [ ] Keep one thing that worked: save it as a recipe or a note.
- [ ] Turn one thing that went wrong into a lesson in your notes.
- [ ] Set next week's top three, linked to your goals.
- [ ] Put the review in your calendar, every week, same time.

After four weeks you'll have your own data to judge this by. That's the point.

## The starter template

Eight sections. Every entry has the same shape: a **title**, a free-text **context** (what, why, details), a **date**, and whether it's still **active**. Keeping every section the same shape is what lets any AI read all of it without special setup.

| Section | What goes in it | Links to |
|---|---|---|
| Goals | What you want in the next few months, and why | Projects |
| Projects | Plans that serve a goal, with a status (planned, doing, reviewing, done) | Goals, tasks, notes |
| Tasks | Single steps, with a due date and a priority | Projects |
| Notes | "How I fixed it", how-tos, things you've learned | Whatever relied on them |
| People | Who you work with, what you discussed or promised | Projects, notes |
| Money | Spending, income, and one or two budgets (optional) | Goals |
| Recipes | Your saved ways of doing things: the capture and review recipes, meeting prep | Everything |
| Diary | One line per thing that got done, and who did it (you or the AI) | Everything |

Two rules make it work:

1. **Link work to what it relied on.** When a task used a note, link them. Later you can ask "what did I base that on?"
2. **"Reviewing" is a status, not a wish.** A project goes doing → reviewing → done. It can't skip the review.

## Recipe 1: capture

Save this as a recipe (a saved prompt) in your AI:

> Here's something I want to remember. Decide which section it belongs to (goals, projects, tasks, notes, people, money). Write it with a short title and a context that explains what and why. Link it to anything related that already exists. Then add one line to the diary saying what you saved.

## Recipe 2: weekly review

> Run my weekly review from the record, not from guesses. Read this week's diary, finished tasks and updated projects. Tell me: what got done, what slipped and why, and which goal each item served. Suggest one thing to keep as a recipe and one lesson to save as a note. Propose my top three for next week, linked to my goals. Ask before writing anything.

## Keep it safe

- Your place stays yours. The AI gets in with a key you control, and you can take it back at any time.
- Keep passwords and sensitive money details out of what the AI can read, or behind a separate key.
- If an AI answer sounds confident but isn't from your notes, ask it where it got that from.
