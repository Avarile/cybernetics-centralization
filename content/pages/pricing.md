## How to join

1. **Register.** Tell us what you'd like to keep track of and which AI you use. Registering is free.
2. **A 15-minute call.** We email you within one working day to book it, so we can check Cybernetics fits what you need.
3. **Reserve your seat.** If it's a fit, we send a payment link and a time for your setup. We create your account once you've paid, so it's ready for the call.

## Why 10 seats

Every setup is done by hand, and every account runs on servers we pay for each month. Ten is how many we can set up well this quarter. People who register hear first when more seats open.

## After early access

When we can host members more cheaply, the price for new members will come down and the setup call will become optional. When that happens, founding members move to the new price automatically and keep everything they joined with. You'll never pay more than someone who joins after you.

## Optional: done-for-you migration

**US$299, once.** A second one-hour session for members whose notes live in several places. We bring them all in, build recipes (saved instructions your AI can follow) and agents for the way you work, and run your first weekly review with you.

## What you'll need alongside it

Cybernetics holds your data. It doesn't come with an AI model, because you choose that yourself:

- To use Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or Cursor, you'll need an account with that AI app, on a plan that lets you add custom connectors. The [connect guides](/docs/connect/overview) show where to find the setting in each app.
- The Agentic Platform runs on an API key from the model provider you choose, so you pay that provider directly for what you use.
