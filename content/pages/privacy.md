This policy covers avarile.com: the home page, the blog, the register form and the downloads. The apps themselves (the Data Base, Project Management, the CRM and the Agentic Platform) have their own terms, which you'll get with your access.

Short version: we collect what you type into the register form, we use it to set up your access and to contact you about it, we don't sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

## What we collect

**When you register**, we receive what you enter in the form:

- Your first and last name and email address.
- Optionally, your job title and company.
- Which description fits you best, your team size, and the modules you're interested in.
- Optionally, what you'd hand to an agent first, and how you heard about us.
- Whether you agreed to product updates.

Along with it, we receive the page you registered on, the page that linked you there (the "referrer"), and the campaign that first brought you to the site, if your link had one (for example "blog" or "youtube").

**When you just browse**, the site itself collects nothing about you. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and the site sets no cookies.

## What's stored in your browser

Two small entries, neither of which is sent anywhere unless you register:

| Name | Where | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| `utm` | Local storage | Remembers the campaign that first brought you here, so a registration is credited to it even if you come back later |
| `booted` | Session storage | Remembers that you've seen the startup animation, so it plays once per visit; cleared when you close the tab |

You can clear both at any time in your browser settings.

## How we use it

- To set up your early access and email you about it. This is why we ask for your agreement on the form.
- To tailor the setup to how you work, using the description, team size, modules and use case you gave us.
- To understand which posts and campaigns bring people in, using the page, referrer and campaign details.
- To send you product updates, **only** if you ticked that box. Every update has a way to unsubscribe.

We don't sell your details, share them with advertisers, or use them to train AI models.

## Services that see your visit

- **Cloudflare** sits in front of the site to deliver it quickly and block abuse. Like any network provider it processes your IP address and basic request details to do that. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
- **Google Fonts.** The interactive figure on [/exocortex](/exocortex) loads a font from Google, which means Google receives your IP address when you open that page. No other page does this.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to:

- see the details we hold about you,
- correct them,
- delete them, or
- stop sending you product updates.

Contact us at [YOU: the same contact email] and we'll do it. We'll confirm when it's done. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can also complain to your local privacy regulator.
