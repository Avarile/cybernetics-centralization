Cybernetics is one memory you own, with any AI plugged into it. It's four apps that work alone or together, and every one of them speaks MCP, the standard plug AI apps use to read and write your data.

## The four modules

| Module | Holds | Built on |
|---|---|---|
| **Data Base** | What you know: notes and how-tos, goals and projects, people, money, in linked tables you design | Teable |
| **Project Management** | What you do: projects, cycles and work items, assigned to people or to agents | Plane |
| **CRM** | Who you work with: people, companies, deals, notes and follow-ups | Twenty |
| **Agentic Platform** | Who acts for you: custom agents and skills that use the other three, on any model | LibreChat |

Start with the **Data Base**. It's the foundation, it works on its own, and every guide begins there. Add the others when you need them.

## How they connect

- **Your AI → each module.** Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor or the Agentic Platform connects to a module's MCP endpoint with a key you create and can revoke at any time. See [Connect your AI](/docs/connect/overview).
- **Project Management → Data Base.** Work items can carry Data Base records, so a task arrives with the know-how it needs.
- **CRM → Data Base.** People, companies and deals get a Database tab for attaching records.
- **Agentic Platform → all three.** Agents read your knowledge, pick up work assigned to them, and write back what they did.
