By the end of this guide you'll have one organised place for your goals, projects, tasks and know-how, an AI connected to it, and a first answer that came from your records rather than a guess.

## What you need

- A Data Base account (hosted or self-hosted).
- One AI app that can connect to it: Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex or Cursor. See [Connect your AI](/docs/connect/overview).
- Optional: the three things you keep forgetting or redoing, from [week 0 of the migration](/blog/ai-native-is-the-new-baseline).

## 1. Build your base from schema packs (5 min)

A schema pack is a ready-made set of linked tables. You choose the areas of life and work you want to organise, and the Data Base builds the tables, links and formulas for you.

1. A new account opens on a short setup: choose **Get started**. Otherwise, create a base in your space and choose **Start from schema packs**.
2. Pick **Progress** (goals, projects and tasks) and **Knowledge** (notes and how-tos). With both, notes link to the goals, projects and tasks they support.
3. Personalise: a base name, your timezone, and leave **Starter rows** on so it doesn't feel empty.
4. Under **Extra tables**, add the **Project frameworks library** (OKR, PDCA and meeting playbooks). The weekly review guide uses it.
5. Review what will be built, then build it.

:::tip Start small
You can add **People** and **Finance** later from the same dialog (**Add schema packs**). Two packs is plenty for day one.
:::

## 2. Connect an AI (10 min)

1. Create a personal access token for the Data Base: [Create access tokens](/docs/connect/access-tokens).
2. Add the server to your AI app: [Claude Code](/docs/connect/claude-code), [Claude Desktop](/docs/connect/claude-desktop), [Codex](/docs/connect/codex) or [Cursor](/docs/connect/cursor-and-others).

## 3. Put your first things in (10 min)

Don't type them into tables. Tell the AI, in one message each:

> Add a goal: get fit for the summer hiking trip. It matters because I want to do the full trail, not half of it. Create a project under it with three tasks for this month.

> Save a how-to: to reset the router, hold the back button for ten seconds, wait for the light to go orange, then log in at 192.168.1.1 and re-enter the Wi-Fi name and password.

> I keep having to look up which tax deductions I can claim. Save that as a note, create a task "Sort out tax return", and link the note to the task.

(These are made-up examples. Use your own three things.)

Open the base afterwards. Each one should sit in the right table and be linked where you asked.

## 4. Ask your first real question (5 min)

> What are my active goals, and what's planned under each one?

> How do I reset the router?

## Check it worked

The answers should name your actual goals, projects and notes. Ask "which records did you use for that?" A connected AI can point to them. If it can't, it's guessing: see [Troubleshooting](/docs/connect/troubleshooting).
